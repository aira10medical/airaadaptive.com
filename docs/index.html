AIRA Adaptive Professional connector

Clinical decision support for plastic surgeons: preoperative risk stratification with the Bukret Score and optimization recommendations, for qualified medical professionals. Works with AI assistants that support remote MCP connectors, including Claude.

Pilot: the current environment accepts fictitious cases only. Do not enter real patient data. Results are decision support for the treating professional; they are not a diagnosis and do not replace clinical judgment. Not for patients or emergencies.

Professional review and use of AI

  • For use by physicians and qualified healthcare professionals only. Patients do not access this connector.
  • Every result must be reviewed and interpreted by the treating professional, who keeps full clinical responsibility and may change or disregard it.
  • The surgical eligibility category is preliminary information for medical review, never an authorization to operate.
  • Results are produced by an algorithm and presented through an AI assistant. Tell your patient when AI-assisted decision support was used in their evaluation.

Requirements

  • An AIRA Adaptive professional account, verified by AIRA Adaptive outside the AI assistant, with assessments available.
  • An AI assistant plan that allows connectors.

Connect

  1. In Claude, open Customize → Connectors and add AIRA Adaptive from the directory, or select Add custom connector and enter the URL https://mcp.drbukret.com/functions/v1/aira-adaptive.
  2. Click Connect. You are sent to the AIRA Adaptive sign-in page.
  3. Sign in with your professional account, check the app name and the return address on the consent screen, and click Allow.

Claude asks for your approval before each tool call by default. You can change this per tool in the connector settings.

To disconnect, remove the connector in your assistant's settings. Access tokens expire automatically.

Tools

ToolTypeWhat it does
start_assessmentReadChecks that the account is verified and has an assessment available, and lists the data required. Uses no assessment.
get_available_assessmentsReadReturns the account status and the number of assessments available.
run_assessmentWriteRuns the official AIRA Adaptive engine after you confirm the data and returns the report: Bukret Score, risk level, contributing factors, recommendations and a surgical eligibility suggestion. Uses one assessment only if a report is returned.
list_assessmentsReadLists recent assessments of your organization.
get_assessmentReadReturns one full report from your organization's history.

Example prompts

  • "How many AIRA Adaptive assessments do I have available?"
  • "Fictitious test case TEST-01: run an AIRA Adaptive pre-op screening. 45 y, 70 kg, 168 cm, never smoked, Caprini 2, liposuction, all 3 consents given."
  • "Show my last 5 AIRA Adaptive assessments."
  • "Open the full report of my most recent AIRA Adaptive assessment."

Data

The connector receives only the structured fields of a confirmed assessment: age, weight, height, smoking status, Caprini total score, optional hypertension, procedure and short case alias, plus the three consents as stated by the professional. It never asks for names, emails, phone numbers or ID numbers, and it does not read your assistant's memory or chat history. See the Privacy Policy and Terms.
