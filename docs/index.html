AIRA Adaptive connector

Clinical decision support for personalized preoperative assessment in plastic and aesthetic surgery: Bukret Score risk stratification and optimization recommendations, for licensed physicians. Works with AI assistants that support remote MCP connectors, including Claude.

Activation status: processing of patient data is pending activation while regulatory and data-protection requirements are completed. Until then, the server asks the professional to confirm that each case is fictitious. Do not enter an actual patient's data until this notice is removed. Results are decision support for the treating professional; they are not a diagnosis and do not replace clinical judgment. Not for patients or emergencies.

Professional review and use of AI

  • For use by physicians and qualified healthcare professionals only. The physician is the user: patients never use this connector or run their own assessment.
  • The physician obtains and documents the patient's consents, enters and validates the clinical data, runs the assessment, interprets the Bukret Score and risk factors, reviews the recommendations and makes the final decision on surgical eligibility and preoperative optimization.
  • Every result must be reviewed and interpreted by the treating professional, who keeps full clinical responsibility and may change or disregard it.
  • The surgical eligibility category is preliminary information for medical review, never an authorization to operate.
  • Results are produced by an algorithm and presented through an AI assistant. Tell your patient when AI-assisted decision support was used in their evaluation.

See also: Intended use · Clinical user guide · Security

Requirements

  • An AIRA Adaptive professional account, verified by AIRA Adaptive outside the AI assistant, with assessments available.
  • An AI assistant plan that allows connectors.

Connect

  1. In Claude, open Customize → Connectors and add AIRA Adaptive from the directory, or select Add custom connector and enter the URL https://mcp.drbukret.com/functions/v1/aira-adaptive.
  2. Click Connect. You are sent to the AIRA Adaptive sign-in page.
  3. Sign in with your professional account, check the app name on the consent screen, and click Allow.

Claude asks for your approval before each tool call by default. You can change this per tool in the connector settings.

To disconnect, remove the connector in your assistant's settings. Access tokens expire automatically.

Consents

Before running, the professional confirms four consents that they obtained from the patient and documented in their own records. Without all four, no assessment is run.

  • Privacy: the patient accepted the AIRA Adaptive privacy policy, including processing in the United States.
  • Medical: the patient gave medical consent.
  • Contact: the patient agreed to be contacted.
  • AI use: the patient was informed and agreed that an AI-assisted decision-support tool is used in their evaluation.

Each completed or failed assessment records, in the organization's audit log, the consents confirmed, the professional who confirmed them, the time and the version of the privacy notice.

Data and validation

The connector receives only the structured fields of a confirmed assessment: age, weight, height, smoking status, Caprini total score, optional hypertension and a procedure from a fixed list (liposuction, abdominoplasty, full facelift, rhinoplasty, breast augmentation or other), plus the four consents. It does not request or accept names, emails, phone numbers, ID numbers, dates or case aliases. History is shown to the assistant with the year only. It does not read your assistant's memory or chat history.

The server rejects: ages outside 16 to 89 or with decimals, a Caprini total with decimals or outside 0 to 60, a weight and height that give a body mass index outside 12 to 80 (usually swapped values), and any procedure outside the list.

Troubleshooting

MessageWhat to do
Account pending verificationYour professional account has not been activated yet. Contact AIRA Adaptive support.
No assessments availableYour organization has no assessments left. Access is managed outside the assistant.
"This connection request is no longer valid"The sign-in link expired. Start the connection again from your assistant.
Patient-data processing pending activationConfirm that the case is fictitious. Processing of patient data is not yet activated.
Missing consents or missing AI consentConfirm all four consents. Without them no assessment is run and no assessment is used.
Implausible BMI, whole numbers, invalid procedureCheck weight and height, enter age and Caprini as whole numbers, and choose a procedure from the list (or "other").
Authorization failed or connector disconnectedRemove and add the connector again, then sign in.
