AIRA Adaptive Professional

Clinical decision support for the personalized preoperative assessment of patients, based on published research and clinical validation

AIRA Adaptive Professional is a clinical decision-support platform for licensed physicians, developed from the peer-reviewed research of Dr. Williams Erik Bukret. It runs a structured preoperative risk stratification with the Bukret Score and returns the report of the AIRA Adaptive engine: risk level, contributing factors, preoperative optimization recommendations for the professional and a preliminary surgical eligibility category for medical review.

Results are structured, interpretable findings that support independent professional judgment. AIRA Adaptive does not establish diagnoses, does not decide surgical eligibility and does not replace the treating physician. Every result must be reviewed by a qualified healthcare professional.

Current release: AIRA Adaptive is designed for physicians to assess their real patients. In this release, real-patient processing is not yet enabled: only cases confirmed as fictitious, used for testing, demonstration and review, are accepted. Do not enter real patient data until this notice is removed.

Scientific basis

  • Bukret WE. A Novel Artificial Intelligence-assisted Risk Assessment Model for Preventing Complications in Esthetic Surgery. Plast Reconstr Surg Glob Open 2021;9(7):e3698. doi:10.1097/GOX.0000000000003698 — development of the Bukret Score and internal validation in a retrospective cohort (372 patients): complication rates rose across risk groups.
  • Bukret WE. Artificial Intelligence-assisted Risk Stratification in Aesthetic Surgery: A Prospective Observational Study. Plast Reconstr Surg Glob Open 2025;13(7):e6948. doi:10.1097/GOX.0000000000006948 — prospective evaluation of the stratification workflow in clinical practice (ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06507384).
  • Bukret WE. Prospective Evaluation of an Automated Rule-based Screening Tool for Body Dysmorphic Disorder in Aesthetic Surgery. Plast Reconstr Surg Glob Open 2025;13(10):e7217. doi:10.1097/GOX.0000000000007217 — related research by the same group; BDD screening is not part of the current AIRA Adaptive release.

External, multicenter validation has not yet been published. No accuracy figure is claimed as clinical performance.

Who it is for

Physicians and surgical teams with an AIRA Adaptive professional account, verified outside the AI assistant (apply for access). Patients never use AIRA Adaptive or run their own assessment. It is not intended for emergencies or diagnosis.

The physician decides

The physician obtains and documents the patient's consents, enters and validates the clinical data, runs the assessment, interprets the Bukret Score and risk factors, reviews the recommendations and makes the final decision on surgical eligibility and preoperative optimization. The software supports that decision; it does not replace clinical judgment or authorize surgery.

Privacy by design

Patient names, emails, phone numbers, ID numbers, dates and case aliases are never requested. The procedure is chosen from a fixed list and history shows the year only. See the Privacy Policy.
