Structured preoperative risk screening for plastic and aesthetic surgery

AIRA Adaptive helps licensed medical professionals run a structured preoperative risk screening and receive the report of the official AIRA Adaptive engine: Bukret Score and risk level, contributing factors, clinical recommendations and a surgical eligibility suggestion pending physician review.

Pilot: the current version accepts fictitious cases only. Do not enter real patient data.

Use it from your AI assistant

AIRA Adaptive is available as a remote MCP connector. In Claude, add it as a connector with the server URL below and sign in with your AIRA Adaptive professional account.

https://mcp.drbukret.com/functions/v1/aira-adaptive

Read the connector documentation

Who it is for

Physicians and surgical teams with an AIRA Adaptive professional account, verified outside the AI assistant. AIRA Adaptive is not intended for patients, emergencies or diagnosis, and its results do not replace the clinical judgment of the treating professional.

Privacy by design

Patient names, emails, phone numbers and ID numbers are never requested. Cases are identified only by an optional short alias. See the Privacy Policy.
