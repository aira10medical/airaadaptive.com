Clinical user guide

For physicians and qualified healthcare professionals · Version 2026-10-24

Current release: AIRA Adaptive is designed for physicians to assess their real patients. In this release, real-patient processing is not yet enabled: the server accepts only cases the professional confirms as fictitious, used for testing, demonstration and review. Do not enter real patient data until this notice is removed.

Before the assessment

  1. Confirm the case is within the intended use: elective plastic or aesthetic surgery, patient aged 16 to 89.
  2. Inform the patient and obtain and document four consents in your own records: privacy (including processing in the United States), medical, contact, and the use of an AI-assisted decision-support tool.
  3. Gather the inputs: age, weight (kg), height (cm), smoking status, Caprini total score, hypertension (optional) and the planned procedure.
  4. Keep the link to the patient in your own medical record. Never type names, contact details, ID numbers or dates into the assistant.

Running the assessment

  1. Ask the assistant for an AIRA Adaptive assessment. It first checks your account and available assessments, and shows a data notice.
  2. Answer one question at a time. Smoking status: never, occasionally, light (1–7 per week), heavy (more than 7 per week) or smoker if the intensity is unknown.
  3. Confirm the four consents only if they were actually given.
  4. Check the summary carefully and confirm. One assessment is used only if a report is returned.

Reading the report

  • Bukret Score and risk level: a structured estimate of preoperative risk from the inputs entered.
  • Contributing factors: which inputs raised the risk.
  • Recommendations: optimization measures to consider, such as weight management, smoking cessation, blood-pressure control or specialist referral.
  • Preliminary surgical eligibility: a category for your review, never an authorization to operate.

Interpret the report together with the full clinical picture. You may change or disregard any result. Record your own decision in the medical record and tell the patient that AI-assisted decision support was used.

History

You can list and reopen assessments of your own organization. History shows the procedure, the year, the score and the risk level.

When not to rely on the result

  • Data you are unsure of, or values that do not match the patient.
  • Conditions not captured by the inputs that change the risk.
  • Any emergency: contact emergency services.

Errors and support

See Troubleshooting. Report unexpected results or safety concerns to drbukret@drbukret.com, including the assessment ID (never patient identifiers).
