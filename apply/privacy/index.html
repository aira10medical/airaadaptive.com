Who is responsible

What we collect

Only the professional contact details you enter in the application form: name, professional email, country, city, institution or practice, specialty, website, approximate surgical volume, your answers about structured risk assessment and demonstrations, your comments, your area of interest and your consent. Applications are kept while the application or the resulting collaboration is being evaluated. You can ask to access, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to drbukret@drbukret.com from the email address you used in the form.
